地图题

考察要点

Overview · 概括扩建方向和保留区域

主建筑向右延伸，较大的演播室和食堂扩张；左侧化妆间、更衣室、接待区仍在原位。

The building has been extended to the right, while several rooms on the left remain in their original locations.

Details 1 · 上半图写扩大和迁移

大演播室占用原布景室、服装室的空间，这两间房迁入右侧原有树木的区域。

The main studio has expanded into the space previously occupied by the scenery and costume rooms.

Details 2 · 下半图写功能替换

小演播室和等候区向右迁移，新食堂占用它们及原大办公室的空间；旧食堂改办公室，停车场向右延伸。

The old canteen has been converted into an office, and the car park has been extended to the right.

In the upper section, the main studio has expanded into the space previously occupied by the scenery and costume rooms.

句式骨架
[现有设施] has expanded into the space previously occupied by [原设施].

The library has expanded into the space previously occupied by the archive.

图书馆扩展到原档案室所在的空间。

The gym has expanded into the space previously occupied by the changing rooms.

健身房扩展到原更衣室所在的空间。

The station hall has expanded into the space previously occupied by the ticket offices.

车站大厅扩展到原售票处所在的空间。

2

改建一处，同时保留其余设施

The old canteen has been converted into an office, while the two offices in the upper section have been retained.

原来的食堂已改建成办公室，而上方区域的两间办公室则保留了下来。

句式骨架
[旧设施] has been converted into [新用途], while [其他设施] have been retained.

The former ticket office has been converted into a café, while the two entrances have been retained.

旧售票处改成了咖啡馆，两个入口得以保留。

The old gym has been converted into an auditorium, while the nearby classrooms have been retained.

旧体育馆已改为礼堂，附近教室仍保留。

The old depot has been converted into a workshop, while the offices have been retained.

旧仓库已改为工坊，办公室仍保留。
