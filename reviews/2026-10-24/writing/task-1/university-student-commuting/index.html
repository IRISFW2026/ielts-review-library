两组等幅、反向变化

By 2019, the car share had fallen by 15 percentage points to 25%, whereas bus use had risen by the same amount to 35%.

到2019年，私家车占比已下降15个百分点至25%，而公交使用占比则上升了相同幅度，达到35%。

句式骨架
[A] fell by [N] percentage points to [X]%, whereas [B] rose by the same amount to [Y]%.

The car share fell by eight percentage points to 22%, whereas rail use rose by the same amount to 30%.

私家车占比下降 8 个百分点至 22%，铁路出行占比等幅升至 30%。

The share of coal fell by ten percentage points to 20%, whereas that of wind rose by the same amount to 25%.

煤炭占比下降 10 个百分点至 20%，风能占比等幅升至 25%。

In-store sales fell by six percentage points to 44%, whereas online sales rose by the same amount to 56% of the total.

门店销售份额下降 6 个百分点至 44%，线上销售份额等幅升至 56%。

2

从落后变为领先

This left buses ahead of cars by ten percentage points, reversing the ranking of these two modes.

这使公交车领先私家车十个百分点，逆转了这两种方式的排名。

句式骨架
This left [新领先组] ahead of [原领先组] by [差距], reversing their previous ranking.

This left rail ahead of cars by eight percentage points, reversing the previous ranking of these two modes.

铁路由此反超私家车，领先 8 个百分点，两种交通方式的排名互换。

This left wind ahead of coal by five percentage points, reversing the ranking of the two sources.

风能由此反超煤炭，领先 5 个百分点，两种能源的排名互换。

This left online sales ahead of in-store sales by twelve percentage points, reversing the previous ranking of the two channels.

线上销售份额由此反超门店销售，领先 12 个百分点，两种渠道的排名互换。
