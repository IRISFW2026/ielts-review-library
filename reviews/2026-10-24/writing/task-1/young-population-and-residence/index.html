考察要点

Overview · 同时概括两个分母

18–34 岁占全国人口的比例先升后降，35% 到约 38% 再到 30%。这一年龄组内部在城市和城镇居住的份额由 75% 升至 95%。

The national share of young adults rose initially before falling, while this age group became more concentrated in cities and towns.

Details 1 · 以峰值为分界

先写 1978 年 35% 到 1992 年约 38%，再写 1998 年 34%、2006 年 32%、2011 年 30%。

The national figure rose to a peak of about 38% in 1992 before falling to 30% in 2011.

Details 2 · 按三类居住地对照两年

城市 55%→65%，城镇 20%→30%，农村 25%→5%。用城市和城镇合计的变化收束。

The shares living in cities and towns increased by ten percentage points each, while the rural share fell by twenty.

1

先升后降，最终低于起点

Overall, the share of young adults rose initially before declining to below its starting level.

总体而言，年轻成年人的占比起初上升，随后下降至低于起始水平。

句式骨架
[指标] rose initially before declining to below its starting level.

The unemployment rate rose initially before declining to below its starting level.

失业率最初上升，随后跌到起点以下。

The proportion of journeys made by bus rose initially before declining to below its starting level.

公交出行的占比先升，随后降至初始水平以下。

Annual water use rose initially before declining to below its starting level.

年度用水量先上升，随后降至初始水平以下。

2

仍然居首，同时占比增加

Cities remained the most common place of residence among 18–34-year-olds, with their share increasing from 55% to 65%.

城市始终是18–34岁人群中最常见的居住地，其占比从55%上升至65%。

句式骨架
[组别] remained the most common [类别], with its share increasing from [起值] to [终值].

Buses remained the most common mode of travel, with their share increasing from 42% to 48%.

公交仍是最常用的出行方式，占比从 42% 升至 48%。

Solar remained the most common source of renewable electricity, with its share increasing from 38% to 44%.

太阳能仍是最常见的可再生电力来源，占比从 38% 升至 44%。

Apartments remained the most common form of accommodation, with their share increasing from 51% to 58%.

公寓仍是最常见的居住形式，占比从 51% 升至 58%。
