1102-写作-Task 1-University Development 大学校园规划

地图题

考察要点

Overview · 概括两条主线

右侧扩充教学设施，新建科学实验楼；左侧重新安排配套设施，增设咖啡馆、图书馆和室内体育馆。

Overall, the campus will have more teaching space and a wider range of facilities.

Details 1 · 写清左侧的两处用途替换

停车场原址改为咖啡馆和图书馆；室外运动场缩至约原来的一半，腾出的空间用于建室内体育馆。

The car park will be replaced by a café and a library. The outdoor sports fields will be reduced to about half their current size to make room for indoor sports halls.

Details 2 · 解释右侧局部减少、整体增加

原教学楼从 11 间减到 10 间，新实验楼增加 5 间教室，使全校总数达到 15 间。实验楼建在原来有树的位置。

Although the existing teaching block will lose one classroom, the new science lab will add five, bringing the campus total to fifteen.

有篇幅可补充行政办公室保留并增设接待区、教职员室扩大，以及实验楼设电脑室。

1

一处减少，另一处新增，总数上升

Although the existing teaching block will lose one classroom, the new science lab will add five, bringing the total number of classrooms on campus from eleven to fifteen.

虽然现有教学楼将减少一间教室，但新实验楼将增加五间，使全校教室总数从十一间增至十五间。

句式骨架
Although [原设施] will lose [减少量], [新设施] will add [新增量], bringing the total number of [同类事物] from [原总数] to [新总数].

Although the main car park will lose ten spaces, a new underground car park will add thirty, bringing the total number of parking spaces from eighty to one hundred.

主停车场将减少十个车位，但新地下停车场将增加三十个，使车位总数从八十个增至一百个。

Although the old clinic will lose two consulting rooms, the new wing will add six, bringing the total number of consulting rooms from twelve to sixteen.

旧诊所将减少两间诊室，但新侧楼将增加六间，使诊室总数从十二间增至十六间。

Although the original terminal will lose one gate, the new terminal will add four, bringing the total number of gates from nine to twelve.

原航站楼将减少一个登机口，但新航站楼将增加四个，使登机口总数从九个增至十二个。

2

原设施缩小，腾出的空间用于新设施

The outdoor sports fields will be reduced to about half their current size to make room for indoor sports halls.

室外运动场将缩至目前面积的约一半，为室内体育馆腾出空间。

句式骨架
[原设施] will be reduced to [缩减后的比例] of its/their current size to make room for [新设施].

The public garden will be reduced to about two-thirds of its current size to make room for a new playground.

公共花园将缩至目前面积的约三分之二，为新游乐场腾出空间。

The market square will be reduced to half its current size to make room for a bus station.

集市广场将缩至目前面积的一半，为公交车站腾出空间。

The outdoor storage area will be reduced to about a third of its current size to make room for a loading bay.

室外仓储区将缩至目前面积的约三分之一，为装卸区腾出空间。

3

一处旧址改为两种用途，再交代相对位置

The neighbouring car park will be replaced by a café and a library, with the café to the left of the library.

相邻的停车场将改成咖啡馆和图书馆，咖啡馆位于图书馆左侧。

句式骨架
[旧设施] will be replaced by [新设施 A] and [新设施 B], with [A] [相对于 B 的位置].

The old warehouse will be replaced by a gym and a swimming pool, with the gym to the left of the pool.

旧仓库将改成健身房和游泳池，健身房位于泳池左侧。

The former bus depot will be replaced by a park and a playground, with the playground behind the park.

原公交车场将改成公园和游乐场，游乐场位于公园后方。

The ticket hall will be replaced by a café and a waiting room, with the café to the right of the waiting room.

售票大厅将改成咖啡馆和候车室，咖啡馆位于候车室右侧。

4

主体留在原位，内部增设设施

The administration office will remain in place, with a reception area added in its upper-right corner.

行政办公室将保留在原位，并在其右上角增设接待区。

句式骨架
[保留的设施] will remain in place, with [新增项目] added [内部位置].

The library will remain in place, with a study area added in its lower-left corner.

图书馆将保留在原位，并在其左下角增设自习区。

The station building will remain in place, with a ticket machine added inside the main entrance.

车站大楼将保留在原位，并在主入口内增设一台售票机。

The museum will remain in place, with a gift shop added near the main entrance.

博物馆将保留在原位，并在主入口附近增设纪念品商店。

5

用现有用途定位新建筑

A new science lab will be built on the site currently occupied by trees.

一座新的科学实验楼将建在目前长有树木的地块上。

句式骨架
[新建筑] will be built on the site currently occupied by [现有设施或地物].

A health centre will be built on the site currently occupied by the car park.

一座医疗中心将建在目前停车场所在的地块上。

A student residence will be built on the site currently occupied by the tennis courts.

一栋学生宿舍将建在目前网球场所在的地块上。

A community centre will be built on the site currently occupied by an old factory.

一座社区中心将建在目前旧工厂所在的地块上。
