Introduction · 同时回应两问

点出工作要求和个人时间管理两类障碍，并引出雇主与个人需要采取的行动。

The main obstacles are excessive work demands and difficulties in managing personal time.

Body 1 · 把困难写成因果链

用加班、晚间消息和长通勤解释时间与精力为什么减少，再把晚间刷手机写成具体情境。

Long commutes make matters worse by using up time and energy that could otherwise be spent on personal interests.

过量工作对应合理工作量和下班后沟通边界，通勤对应适当居家办公，照护冲突对应弹性工时与分担责任。

When family emergencies arise, flexible working hours and shared caring duties can help people manage competing demands.

1

解释某因素怎样加剧问题

Long commutes make matters worse by using up time and energy that could otherwise be spent on personal interests.

长途通勤会进一步加剧问题，耗掉原本可以用于个人兴趣的时间和精力。

句式骨架
[因素] makes matters worse by using up [资源] that could otherwise be spent on [用途].

Frequent meetings make matters worse by using up time that could otherwise be spent on project work.

频繁开会消耗了原可用于项目工作的时间，让情况更糟。

High rents make matters worse by using up income that could otherwise be spent on training.

高房租消耗了原可用于培训的收入，让情况更糟。

Long waits make matters worse by using up time that could otherwise be spent caring for patients.

漫长等待占用了本可用于照护病人的时间，让情况更糟。

2

承认措施有效，再说明解决范围

Employees can improve their working methods to reduce avoidable overtime, although greater efficiency alone cannot solve excessive workloads.

员工可以改进工作方法，减少本可避免的加班，尽管单靠提高效率无法解决过量工作。

句式骨架
[主体] can [做法] to reduce [问题的一部分], although [做法] alone cannot solve [更大的问题].

Students can plan their week to reduce missed deadlines, although planning alone cannot solve an excessive workload.

学生可以规划一周以减少错过截止日期的情况，但仅靠计划无法解决任务过多的问题。

Cities can improve bus routes to reduce congestion, although better buses alone cannot solve the shortage of affordable housing near jobs.

城市可以优化公交线路以缓解拥堵，但单靠公交无法解决工作地点附近平价住房短缺。

Families can insulate their homes to reduce heating costs, although insulation alone cannot solve rising energy prices.

家庭可以改善住房保温来降低取暖成本，但仅靠保温无法解决能源价格上涨。
