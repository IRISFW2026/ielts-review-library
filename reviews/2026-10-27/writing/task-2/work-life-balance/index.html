IELTS WRITING TASK 2

Work–Life Balance｜工作与生活的平衡

问题与解决 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

People aim to keep a balance between their work and other parts of their lives, but few people achieve it. What are the problems in trying to achieve this goal and how can these problems be overcome?

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

问题与解决工作与商业

01 审题判断

题型：问题与解决

核心立场：需要同时从工作制度与个人时间管理入手；不能把结构性工作压力完全归咎于个人。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 问题一：工作延伸至下班后，加班与即时消息侵占个人时间。
  • 问题二：长通勤、疲劳、临时照护责任和缺乏计划进一步压缩休闲时间。
  • 解决一：雇主设定合理工作量、明确非工作时间边界，并提供适度弹性。
  • 解决二：个人减少无目的手机使用，提前安排休闲活动；家庭成员分担照护责任。

03 考场作答指南

  • 题目要求讨论 problems 及 how they can be overcome，两个任务都必须明确回答。
  • 问题段写清楚工作要求如何吞噬时间与精力，不要只罗列“忙”。
  • 解决段要与前文问题一一对应：工作边界对应加班，弹性安排对应突发照护，个人计划对应无目的消磨时间。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 改写工作与生活难以平衡的现象；概括主要障碍来自工作要求与个人时间管理。

Body 1

→ 论点：加班／下班后消息占用时间；解释长通勤与疲劳如何减少个人精力；例子：晚间刷手机或临时照护打乱计划。

Body 2

→ 论点：雇主应设合理工作量、非工作时间边界与弹性安排；个人应规划休闲活动、减少无目的手机使用；解释措施如何恢复可支配时间。

Conclusion

→ 总结结构性工作压力与个人安排问题；强调雇主和个人共同采取行动。

完整全文

Introduction

Although many people want a healthy balance between work and personal life, this often proves difficult to achieve. The main obstacles are excessive work demands and difficulties in managing personal time, which require action from both employers and individuals.

Body 1

One major barrier is that work often extends beyond official working hours. Employees may be expected to work overtime or answer messages in the evening, leaving little time for family activities or hobbies. Long commutes make matters worse by using up time and energy that could otherwise be spent on personal interests. Even when free time is available, tired workers may struggle to use it as they intended. For example, someone who plans to watch a few short videos after work may end up scrolling through social media all evening. Unexpected caring responsibilities, such as looking after a sick relative, can also disrupt plans, especially when working arrangements are inflexible.

Body 2

Addressing these problems requires changes in both workplace practices and personal routines. Employers should set realistic workloads and avoid expecting staff to respond to non-urgent messages outside working hours. This would give employees more predictable periods of personal time. Where the job allows it, occasional home working could also reduce commuting time. Employees can improve their working methods to reduce avoidable overtime, although greater efficiency alone cannot solve excessive workloads. To protect their leisure time, individuals could schedule a few enjoyable activities each week and limit aimless phone use. Arranging a walk with a friend, for instance, creates a specific commitment that is easier to keep than a vague intention to relax. When family emergencies arise, flexible working hours and shared caring duties can help people manage competing demands.

Conclusion

In conclusion, demanding jobs, unplanned leisure time and sudden family needs can all prevent people from achieving a satisfactory balance. More supportive employers and realistic personal planning can make this goal more achievable.
