1006-口语-Part 1-Singing 唱歌

Part 1 主题积累｜2026-10-06

目标：短句 + 词块直接组织英语，而不是先想完整中文再翻译。

发音使用浏览器/系统自带英文语音，优先选择 en-GB 英式语音；不同设备可用声音会有所不同。

A. 原始 Part 1 四题

1. Do you like singing? Why?
你喜欢唱歌吗？为什么？
Not really. I'm not very good at singing, and I'm a little tone-deaf. Sometimes I think I'm singing in tune, but apparently I'm not.
关键词： tone-deafsing in tune
查看反馈
tone-deaf = 五音不全；sing in tune = 唱准调。不要说 tongue-deaf 或 sing the right tone。
2. Have you ever learnt how to sing?
你以前学过唱歌吗？
No, I've never had any formal singing lessons. I just sing along to songs I like for fun sometimes. I've never really learned how to sing properly.
关键词： formal singing lessonssing along to songsfor fun
3. Who do you want to sing for?
你想唱给谁听？
Probably just myself. I sometimes sing when I'm bored, but singing in front of other people makes me feel embarrassed. So I prefer singing when I'm alone.
关键词： when I'm boredfeel embarrassedwhen I'm alone
4. Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?
你认为唱歌能给人带来快乐吗？
Yes, definitely, but I don't think singing only brings happiness. Singers can express all kinds of emotions through their voices. Some songs make people feel happy, while others can make them feel sad or emotional.
关键词： express emotionsmake people feel

B. 同主题随机追问

5. When do you usually sing?
你通常什么时候唱歌？
I rarely sing. I think the last time was last year. I couldn't sleep one night, so I stayed up singing until about 3 a.m.
关键词： rarely singcouldn't sleepstay up
6. Do you ever sing with your friends?
你会和朋友一起唱歌吗？
Yes, sometimes. I've been to karaoke with friends and colleagues before. But I only feel comfortable singing around close friends because I'm not very confident about my singing.
7. What kind of music do you like singing?
你喜欢唱什么类型的歌？
I prefer soft and gentle songs, especially love songs or popular songs that are easy to sing along to. I don't listen to music that much, so I usually only know songs that I've heard many times.
8. Would you like to learn singing in the future?
以后想学唱歌吗？
Maybe. I'd like to take singing lessons someday, but probably not now. I don't really have enough time or money for it, and to be honest, singing isn't a major interest of mine.
9. Why do some people enjoy singing so much?
为什么有些人很享受唱歌？
I think one reason is that some people are naturally good at singing, so they get a lot of positive feedback from others. Another reason is that singing helps people express their feelings and release their emotions.

C. 今日高频纠错

❌ tongue-deaf → ✅ tone-deaf

❌ sing the right tone → ✅ sing in tune

❌ I'm not really like singing → ✅ I don't really like singing

❌ listen music → ✅ listen to music

❌ other peoples → ✅ other people

❌ I sing very very less → ✅ I rarely sing / I don't sing very often

❌ learn singing in formal lessons → ✅ take singing lessons / have formal singing lessons

D. 练习方法

第一轮：只看关键词回答，每题 15–25 秒。

第二轮：播放参考答案，逐句 shadowing，先慢速，再正常速度。

第三轮：隐藏答案，再次回答。允许换句子，不要求背参考答案。
