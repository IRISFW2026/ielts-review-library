Overview · 先写长期领跑者，再分清三条下降线

服务业全程最大且总体上升。其余三项期末均低于起点，其中矿业一路下降，制造业与农业先升后降。

Services led throughout, while manufacturing and agriculture rose before falling and mining declined continuously.

Details 1 · 用对比组织服务业和矿业

服务业 50%→60%，1940 至 1960 年持平，期末达到 70%；矿业从约 7% 降至 0%。

Services remained at 60% between 1940 and 1960, whereas mining fell to zero by 2000.

Details 2 · 比较两个峰值及期末收敛

制造业约 1920 年达到 28%，农业约 1940 年达到 15%；期末分别约为 10% 和 8%。

Manufacturing peaked earlier and at a higher level than agriculture, but their shares were much closer by 2000.

先升到平台，再继续上升

The figure for services reached 60% in 1940 and remained unchanged until 1960, before rising to 70% in 2000.

Manufacturing rose from about 18% in 1900 to a peak of roughly 28% in 1920, then declined to 10% in 2000.

制造业的就业占比从1900年的约18%上升至1920年约28%的峰值，随后在2000年降至10%。
