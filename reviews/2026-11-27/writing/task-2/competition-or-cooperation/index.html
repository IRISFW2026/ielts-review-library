1127-写作-大作文-Competition or Cooperation 竞争与合作

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Some people think that competition at work, at school and in daily life is a good thing. Others believe that we should try to cooperate more, rather than competing against each other. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

双边讨论

社会与家庭

审题判断

题目横跨工作、学校和日常生活。无需每个场景都写一遍，但例子应体现竞争与合作的不同机制。

建议立场

竞争能推动个人努力和创新，但过度竞争会损害信任；总体更支持以合作为主、适度竞争作为激励。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 竞争：设定目标、提高效率、识别优秀表现；适度竞争可激发努力。
  • 合作：共享知识、互补技能、共同解决复杂问题；有助于建立信任。
  • 风险：零和心态、信息囤积、作弊和压力。立场：合作应为主，竞争需有边界。

本题考场提醒

  • 写竞争的正面作用时强调“适度”，否则会与后文风险矛盾。
  • 合作段要解释互补技能和信息共享如何改善结果。
  • 明确给出个人观点，不要只说两者都重要。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

呈现两种观点并表明合作更重要，适度竞争可作为补充。

Body 1

解释竞争如何激励努力、提升标准，并指出过度竞争的风险。

Body 2

论证合作如何汇集不同能力、解决复杂任务和建立信任。

Conclusion

总结合作应成为主要原则，竞争只在公平规则下发挥作用。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

Fair competition can raise performance standards.

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Competition and cooperation both influence how people behave at school, at work and in everyday life. While competition can encourage individuals to improve, I believe cooperation is more valuable overall because many important goals require people to work together.

Body 1

Supporters of competition argue that it gives people a reason to work harder. In schools, for example, academic contests may encourage students to revise thoroughly and aim for higher standards. In businesses, competition between companies can lead to better products and more efficient services. It can also help individuals recognise their strengths and identify areas where they need to improve. However, these benefits depend on fair rules. If winning becomes the only priority, people may hide information, take unfair shortcuts or view colleagues as threats rather than partners.

Body 2

Cooperation, by contrast, enables people to combine different abilities and solve problems that would be difficult for one person to handle alone. A workplace team may include members with technical, creative and organisational skills, and sharing their knowledge can produce a stronger result than isolated efforts. In education, students who explain ideas to one another can deepen their understanding while learning to respect different viewpoints. Cooperation also builds trust, which is important for long-term relationships and community life. For these reasons, I believe it should be the main principle, although limited competition can still provide useful motivation.

Conclusion

In conclusion, competition can raise standards when it remains fair and moderate, but cooperation is more effective for complex tasks and lasting relationships. Society should encourage people to improve while making shared success more important than defeating others.

本题复盘重点

最稳妥的比较不是竞争坏、合作好，而是竞争适合设定标准，合作适合处理复杂任务。用“适度竞争、合作优先”形成明确立场。
