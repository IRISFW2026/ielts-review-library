1127-写作-大作文-Everyday Stress 日常生活压力

问题与解决 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

Everyday activities cause stress for many people in the modern world. Why is this the case? What can be done to solve this problem?

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

问题与解决问题与解决

01 审题判断

题型：问题与解决

核心立场：压力主要来自时间竞争、经济不确定性和持续的信息干扰；解决需要个人安排与组织、公共服务共同介入。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 原因：工作与家庭角色叠加、通勤和时间紧迫；生活成本和就业不确定；手机消息造成持续打断。
  • 个人：优先排序、安排休息、减少非必要通知、寻求支持。
  • 雇主与政府：合理工作量、弹性安排、可及心理支持和公共交通服务。

03 考场作答指南

  • 原因至少区分时间、经济或信息因素，避免同义重复。
  • 解决方案要逐条对应原因，不要只写“放松心情”。
  • 避免把结构性问题全部交给个人自律。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 概括现代日常压力并指出多种结构性与个人因素。

Body 1

→ 解释时间压力、经济压力和信息过载如何累积。

Body 2

→ 按原因对应提出个人、雇主和公共层面的措施。

Conclusion

→ 总结缓解压力需要个人习惯与制度支持配合。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向，考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

完整全文

Introduction

Many people experience stress while carrying out ordinary activities in modern life. This is largely because work, family and financial demands compete for limited time, while digital technology makes it harder to switch off. Solutions are needed from both individuals and institutions.

Body 1

A major cause is the pressure to fulfil several responsibilities within a limited day. Employees may face tight deadlines and long commutes, while also caring for children or older relatives. These competing demands can leave little time for rest. Financial uncertainty is another source of stress. Rising housing and living costs may force people to work longer hours or worry constantly about their income. In addition, smartphones and online platforms create frequent interruptions. Messages from work, news updates and social media can make people feel that they must remain available even when they are not working.

Body 2

Individuals can reduce some pressure by identifying priorities, planning realistic schedules and setting limits on non-essential phone notifications. Regular exercise and social support may also help people recover from demanding periods. However, personal effort cannot solve every cause. Employers should distribute tasks fairly, avoid unnecessary overtime and allow flexible arrangements where possible. Governments can contribute by improving public transport, expanding access to affordable childcare and ensuring that mental health support is available. These measures reduce the practical pressures that individuals cannot control on their own.

Conclusion

In conclusion, everyday stress is driven by overlapping responsibilities, financial concerns and constant digital demands. Better personal routines can help, but lasting improvements also require supportive workplaces and public services.
