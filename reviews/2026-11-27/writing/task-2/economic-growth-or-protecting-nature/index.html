1127-写作-大作文-Economic Growth or Protecting Nature 经济发展与自然保护

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Some people think that economic development should be the most important goal of governments, while others argue that protecting the natural world is more important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

双边讨论

政府与法律

审题判断

讨论政府目标的优先次序。两边都要有合理理由，自己的观点要解释如何处理冲突，而非简单说两者都重要。

建议立场

支持可持续发展：经济发展提供就业和公共财政，但自然环境是长期生产与健康的基础，政府不应以不可逆生态损失换取短期增长。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 经济优先：创造就业、提高收入、改善基础设施和公共服务；贫困地区需要增长。
  • 自然优先：生态系统支撑农业、水源、健康与防灾；破坏可能不可逆并造成长期成本。
  • 立场：增长必须受环境底线约束，优先投资低碳产业和绿色基础设施。

本题考场提醒

  • 不要把经济增长只写成企业利润，关联就业、税收和公共服务。
  • 把自然保护与经济长期稳定联系起来，避免只诉诸道德。
  • 观点需要有优先级：不可逆生态风险应设为政策边界。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

介绍两种政策优先级，并提出环境底线下的可持续增长立场。

Body 1

解释经济发展对就业、税收和生活水平的贡献。

Body 2

说明自然资本的长期价值与生态破坏成本，提出绿色增长方向。

Conclusion

总结政府应追求受环境约束的长期繁荣。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Governments are often expected to promote economic growth and protect the natural environment, yet these goals can sometimes conflict. Although economic development is essential for improving living standards, I believe environmental protection should set clear limits on how growth is pursued.

Body 1

Prioritising economic development can be justified, particularly in countries where poverty and unemployment remain serious problems. New industries and infrastructure projects can generate employment opportunities, raise household incomes and improve access to transport, education and healthcare. A growing economy also produces public revenue, which governments can use to fund essential services. For communities with few job prospects, refusing all development may preserve the landscape but leave residents with limited opportunities. Economic progress is therefore an important means of improving people's quality of life.

Body 2

However, economic activity depends on natural resources and stable ecosystems. Forests regulate water supplies, fertile soil supports agriculture and clean air is essential for public health. If these resources are damaged for short-term gains, the resulting costs may be difficult or impossible to reverse. Pollution can increase healthcare spending, while floods and droughts may destroy homes and infrastructure. In my view, governments should therefore require development projects to meet environmental standards and direct investment towards renewable energy, efficient public transport and cleaner production. This allows economic activity to continue without treating nature as an unlimited resource.

Conclusion

In conclusion, economic growth can reduce poverty and finance public services, but it cannot be sustainable if it destroys the natural systems on which society depends. Governments should pursue long-term prosperity within firm environmental limits.

本题复盘重点

两边的论证要放在不同时间尺度上比较：经济增长带来较直接的就业和收入，生态保护维护长期生产基础与健康。
