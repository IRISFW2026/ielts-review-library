1127-写作-大作文-Group Study or Self-Study 小组学习与自主学习

双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

Some people think studying in groups is better, while some think self-study is better for school students. Discuss both sides and give your opinion.

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

双边讨论双边讨论

01 审题判断

题型：双边讨论

核心立场：两种方式各有作用；若必须选择主要学习方式，自主学习更适合建立独立理解，小组学习适合讨论和查漏补缺。

审题关键词：两种方式各有作用；若必须选择主要学习方式，自主学习更适合建立独立理解，小组学习适合讨论和查漏补缺。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 小组：解释概念、比较解题方法、获得反馈、练习表达。
  • 独学：按个人节奏安排、专注薄弱点、培养自律和独立思考。
  • 立场：独学为基础，讨论作为补充；教师可安排有目标的小组任务。

03 考场作答指南

  • 两边各写一个清晰机制，不要只列优点形容词。
  • 用同一学习目标比较两种方式，例如理解难题或复习薄弱知识。
  • 观点要明确回答“which is better”，避免结尾只说因人而异。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。
  • 每个主体段遵循“观点 → 解释 → 具体例子／结果”，避免只罗列观点。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 引出两种学习方式，并表明自主学习应为基础、小组学习作补充。

Body 1

→ 解释小组学习如何通过观点交流和同伴反馈深化理解。

Body 2

→ 说明独学的节奏与专注优势，并提出组合使用的方式。

Conclusion

→ 总结两者互补，强调独立学习是基础。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向；考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

Introduction

There is disagreement about whether school students learn more effectively in groups or on their own. Although group study offers valuable opportunities for discussion, I believe that self-study should form the foundation of learning, with group work used as a supplement.

Body 1

Studying with others can make learning more interactive. When students discuss a difficult topic, they may encounter explanations and approaches that they would not have considered independently. A classmate who understands a mathematical problem, for example, can explain it in simpler language, while questions from other group members may reveal weaknesses in the explanation. Group tasks also encourage students to express ideas clearly and listen to different opinions. These skills are useful beyond school, particularly in workplaces where people are expected to cooperate.

Body 2

However, self-study gives learners greater control over their time and attention. Students do not all understand new material at the same speed, and independent work allows them to spend longer on difficult areas instead of following the pace of a group. It also requires them to identify what they do not understand, find relevant information and monitor their own progress. These habits are essential for long-term learning. In my view, students should first review material independently and then use carefully planned group sessions to compare answers or discuss challenging questions. This arrangement combines concentration with peer feedback.

Conclusion

In conclusion, group study is valuable for exchanging ideas and checking understanding, but self-study is more important as the basis of academic progress. The most effective approach is to develop independent learning habits and use group work selectively.
