1127-写作-大作文-Laws That Are Hard to Enforce 难以执行的法律

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 问题与解决 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Certain laws have little effect now because it is difficult for the police to enforce them. Why is that? How can this problem be solved?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

问题与解决

政府与法律

审题判断

题目要求解释法律为何难以执行，以及如何解决。注意“法律本身无效”与“执行困难”不同，原因应聚焦发现、取证和执行能力。

建议立场

执法困难来自违法行为隐蔽、资源有限、规则不清和公众不配合；解决要提升可执行性、技术与社区合作，而非单纯加重处罚。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 原因：违规行为分散隐蔽；警力和预算有限；证据难收集；法规复杂或公众不理解。
  • 措施：简化规则、明确责任和处罚；培训执法人员并合理配置资源；使用合规技术；公众举报与教育。
  • 边界：技术监控需保护隐私，处罚应与违法程度相称。

本题考场提醒

  • 原因要围绕执法链条：发现—取证—处理，而非泛泛写社会问题。
  • 每项措施对应一个障碍，形成清楚配对。
  • 使用监控技术时补充隐私与监督条件，避免建议过度。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

改写部分法律难以落实的现象，并概括执行层面的障碍。

Body 1

解释隐蔽性、资源不足、证据困难和公众认知不足。

Body 2

提出规则清晰化、资源与技术支持、公众合作和比例处罚。

Conclusion

总结提高可执行性比单纯增加惩罚更关键。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

Police forces may have limited enforcement resources.

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Some laws have little practical effect because the police find it difficult to enforce them. This problem is often caused by limited resources, the hidden nature of certain offences and a lack of public understanding. It can be addressed through clearer rules and better cooperation.

Body 1

One reason is that some violations are difficult to detect. Minor offences may occur in private places or across large areas, making it impossible for police officers to observe every incident. Even when a violation is reported, gathering admissible evidence can take considerable time. A second problem is that police forces may have limited staff and funding, so they must prioritise serious crimes over less urgent breaches. In addition, complicated regulations can make it unclear to the public what is prohibited and to officers how a rule should be applied. If people do not understand a law, voluntary compliance is also likely to be low.

Body 2

Governments should first make laws clearer and ensure that responsibilities and penalties are easy to understand. Police officers also need appropriate training and resources so that investigations can be conducted efficiently. In situations where offences are difficult to observe directly, carefully regulated technology, such as cameras in public spaces, may help collect evidence. However, its use should be proportionate and subject to privacy safeguards. Public education is equally important: explaining why a law exists and how people can report violations may encourage greater cooperation. Finally, penalties should be applied consistently so that people understand that rules are meaningful.

Conclusion

In conclusion, weak enforcement often results from detection difficulties, insufficient resources and unclear rules. Clear legislation, better-trained officers, responsible technology and public cooperation can make laws more effective in practice.

本题复盘重点

问题问的是执法为什么困难，答案要落在发现、取证、资源和理解上。解决方案按这些障碍逐项对应，避免只提出“加大处罚”。
