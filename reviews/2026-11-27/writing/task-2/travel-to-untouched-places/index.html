1127-写作-大作文-Travel to Untouched Places 前往未开发自然地区旅游

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 利弊分析 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Today more and more people like to go to untouched and unexplored natural places to travel. What are the advantages and disadvantages for individuals and the country?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

利弊分析

交通与旅游

审题判断

题目要求分别分析个人和国家层面的利弊。不要只写游客个人体验；国家层面要涉及经济、就业、自然资产与公共管理。

建议立场

对个人有教育、休息和探索价值；对国家可带动偏远地区经济，但环境脆弱、基础设施和救援成本高，必须严格管理。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 个人优势：远离城市压力、亲近自然、提升环保意识和独立能力。
  • 国家优势：带动偏远地区就业、住宿、交通和地方产品；提升自然遗产知名度。
  • 劣势：踩踏、垃圾、野生动物干扰、栖息地破坏；救援与基础设施成本增加。

本题考场提醒

  • 题目明确要求 individuals and the country，至少要覆盖两个层面。
  • 优势与劣势都要出现个人和国家的影响，不要只讲旅游收入。
  • 用限流、指定路线、游客教育和当地社区参与回应环境风险。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

概括自然探险旅游增长，并说明其机会与风险并存。

Body 1

个人层面：身心体验与自然教育；国家层面：偏远地区经济收益。

Body 2

环境和公共管理成本：生态脆弱、游客垃圾、救援设施；提出限流与低影响旅游。

Conclusion

总结收益取决于是否以保护为前提进行管理。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Travelling to remote and relatively untouched natural areas has become increasingly popular. Such trips can benefit individuals and local economies, but they may also damage fragile environments and create costs for national and local authorities.

Body 1

For individuals, visiting unexplored landscapes can provide a welcome break from crowded urban environments. Walking through forests, mountains or coastal areas may improve physical health and reduce stress. These experiences can also increase people's understanding of wildlife and conservation, especially when visitors learn about local ecosystems from trained guides. At a national level, nature tourism can bring income to communities that have few other economic opportunities. Visitors may pay for accommodation, transport, food and guiding services, creating jobs and supporting local businesses.

Body 2

The main disadvantage is that natural areas are often environmentally sensitive. Large numbers of visitors can leave rubbish, disturb wildlife and damage plants or paths. Once habitats are affected, recovery may take many years. Remote destinations may also lack roads, medical facilities and waste-management systems, meaning that governments have to spend money on infrastructure and emergency services. These costs can outweigh the economic benefits if tourism is poorly controlled. Authorities should establish marked routes, limit visitor numbers in vulnerable locations and require tour operators to follow environmental standards. Local communities should also be involved in decisions about tourism development.

Conclusion

In conclusion, travel to untouched places can improve personal well-being and support regional economies, but it carries environmental and public costs. Careful management is essential if these benefits are to be sustainable.

本题复盘重点

本题容易遗漏国家层面负面影响。除环境损害外，可写偏远地区垃圾处理、医疗救援和交通设施的公共成本。
