1127-写作-大作文-Age and Job Applications 年龄与招聘

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 观点题 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

In some countries, it is illegal for companies to reject applicants based on age. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

观点题

工作与商业

审题判断

讨论的是“based on age”作为拒绝理由，而不是所有岗位都完全不考虑年龄。要区分年龄偏见与有客观依据的岗位要求。

建议立场

总体赞成禁止仅凭年龄拒绝申请者；招聘应依据岗位相关能力，同时允许真正与安全或法定资格相关的合理要求。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 反对年龄歧视：年龄不能准确代表技能、体能、学习能力或可靠性。
  • 岗位匹配：经验丰富者可带来判断力；年轻人可能具备新技能，需看个人能力。
  • 限定：少数岗位有法定最低年龄或明确安全要求，但应有证据且范围窄。

本题考场提醒

  • 精准区分年龄本身与岗位能力，避免极端表态。
  • 举例说明不同年龄群体都可能具备岗位所需优势。
  • 例外必须是客观、必要、与工作直接相关，不能泛化。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

表明支持禁止以年龄本身作为拒绝理由，但承认狭窄的法定例外。

Body 1

解释年龄作为筛选捷径为何不公平且不准确。

Body 2

提出能力本位招聘；讨论合法资格或安全标准的有限例外。

Conclusion

总结招聘应以岗位相关能力为核心，年龄不能替代评估。

分段逻辑

先完成题目要求的两项核心任务，再用两个主体段分别展开不同论证方向；每段采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条，避免重复。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

I agree that companies should not be allowed to reject job applicants simply because of their age. Recruitment should focus on a person's ability to perform the role, although a small number of positions may involve genuine legal or safety requirements.

Body 1

Using age as a shortcut for judging applicants is both unfair and unreliable. People of the same age can differ greatly in health, experience, motivation and technical ability. An older candidate may have years of relevant knowledge and strong problem-solving skills, while a younger applicant may bring up-to-date training and familiarity with new technologies. Rejecting either group without assessing their qualifications deprives employers of potentially suitable workers. It can also reinforce stereotypes, such as the assumption that older employees are unwilling to learn or that younger people lack responsibility.

Body 2

A fairer approach is to use transparent, job-related criteria. Employers can assess professional experience, practical tests, communication skills and the ability to meet the actual demands of a position. This allows them to select the most suitable person while giving applicants equal opportunities. There may, however, be limited situations in which age is relevant because of specific legislation or demonstrable safety concerns. Such cases should be clearly defined and supported by evidence, rather than used as a general excuse to exclude candidates. Where a physical requirement exists, for example, employers should normally assess the ability to meet that requirement directly.

Conclusion

In conclusion, I support laws that prevent age-based rejection because age alone is not a reliable measure of competence. Employers should evaluate applicants individually and use age-related restrictions only where they are genuinely necessary and legally justified.

本题复盘重点

立场不是“任何情况下年龄都无关”，而是禁止把年龄当作能力代理指标。有限例外能体现判断的边界。
