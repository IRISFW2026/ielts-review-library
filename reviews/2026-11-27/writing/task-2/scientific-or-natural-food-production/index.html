1127-写作-大作文-Scientific or Natural Food Production 科学种植与自然种植

双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

Some people think that using scientific technique is better for growing food, others think it is better for food grow naturally. Discuss both views.

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

双边讨论双边讨论

01 审题判断

题型：双边讨论

核心立场：科学技术可提高产量、稳定供应并减少损失；自然方式有利于生态与土壤健康。倾向于结合两者，按作物与环境选择低影响技术。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 科学方法：改良种子、精准灌溉、病虫害监测、温室控制，提高产量和抗风险能力。
  • 自然方法：减少化学投入、保护土壤生物多样性、降低水土污染。
  • 综合：采用科学监测和精准技术，同时控制化学投入，推广轮作与土壤保护。

03 考场作答指南

  • 区分“科学技术”与“高强度化学投入”，并非所有科技都伤害环境。
  • 区分“自然种植”与完全不管理；讨论产量和病虫害风险。
  • 用精准灌溉、监测、轮作等具体措施避免空泛折中。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 改写两种食物生产方式的争议。

Body 1

→ 解释科学技术如何提升产量、稳定性和资源使用效率。

Body 2

→ 说明自然种植对生态的好处，并指出其产量和管理限制；可引出折中方案。

Conclusion

→ 总结两种方式各有适用场景，强调兼顾产量与环境。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向，考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

完整全文

Introduction

There are different views about whether food should be grown with scientific techniques or by relying on more natural methods. Both approaches have advantages: technology can improve food production, while natural farming can protect the environment.

Body 1

Supporters of scientific agriculture argue that modern methods are necessary to feed a growing population. Improved seeds, controlled greenhouses and accurate irrigation can increase crop yields and make production less dependent on weather conditions. Technology can also help farmers identify pests and diseases at an early stage, reducing crop losses and allowing them to use water and fertilisers more efficiently. In regions where farmland is limited or rainfall is unreliable, these methods can make food supplies more stable and affordable.

Body 2

On the other hand, natural farming methods are valued because they can reduce the environmental damage associated with intensive agriculture. Avoiding excessive chemical inputs may protect soil organisms, nearby rivers and wildlife. Practices such as crop rotation and composting can also maintain soil quality over time. However, natural methods may require more land or labour and can be vulnerable to pests if they are not managed carefully. It is therefore unhelpful to treat science and nature as complete opposites. Farmers can use scientific monitoring and precision equipment while adopting environmentally responsible practices.

Conclusion

In conclusion, scientific techniques can improve productivity and reliability, whereas natural methods offer important environmental benefits. A balanced approach that applies suitable technology while protecting soil and water is likely to be the most sustainable option.
