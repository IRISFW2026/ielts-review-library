1127-写作-大作文-Watching the Same Films 全世界看同样的电影

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 利弊分析 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

People around the world now watch the same films. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

利弊分析

文化与历史

审题判断

这是利弊分析题，不要求明确选择一边。要分别写跨文化共同话题与文化多样性受压的影响。

建议立场

利弊并存：共同影视作品促进跨文化交流和产业合作，但全球市场集中也可能挤压本土电影和语言文化。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 优势：形成跨国共同话题；观众接触不同文化；国际发行扩大创作者市场。
  • 劣势：大型制作占据院线和平台推荐；本土语言和小众故事曝光减少；文化表达趋同。
  • 平衡：支持本土创作、字幕发行、多元平台推荐和文化基金。

本题考场提醒

  • 优势段要写交流如何发生，不要只说“大家有共同兴趣”。
  • 劣势聚焦发行与平台资源集中如何影响本土作品曝光。
  • 避免把文化交流写成文化同质化；区分接触他者与替代本土文化。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

描述全球观众观看相同电影的现象，并指出有交流与文化多样性两方面影响。

Body 1

解释共同电影如何促进文化交流、共享话题和产业传播。

Body 2

分析市场集中对本土创作、语言和小众故事的挤压。

Conclusion

总结全球传播有益，但需保障本土作品的可见度。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Films produced in a small number of countries are now watched by audiences across the world. This trend can encourage cultural exchange and create shared experiences, but it may also reduce the visibility of local film industries and cultural traditions.

Body 1

One advantage is that internationally popular films give people from different backgrounds a common subject to discuss. Viewers can share opinions about characters and stories even if they live in different countries, which may create opportunities for communication. Films can also introduce audiences to unfamiliar customs, historical events and social issues. In addition, global distribution allows filmmakers to reach a much larger audience than would be possible in their home markets. This can increase revenue and support international cooperation in film production.

Body 2

The main disadvantage is that the global market may become dominated by a small number of powerful studios and streaming platforms. Their productions often receive more advertising, cinema space and recommendations than local films. As a result, smaller industries may struggle to attract funding and audiences, even when their stories are culturally significant. If viewers mainly consume the same international productions, local languages, traditions and perspectives may become less visible in mainstream entertainment. Governments and platforms can reduce this risk by supporting independent filmmaking, providing subtitles and translation, and ensuring that local productions are discoverable.

Conclusion

In conclusion, watching the same films can promote shared experiences and cross-cultural understanding, but it may weaken the diversity of film culture if global distribution is controlled by a few major producers. International access should therefore be balanced with support for local creativity.

本题复盘重点

利弊题要区分“全球共同观看”带来的交流优势和“市场集中”造成的文化风险。不是所有国际电影都会损害本土文化，问题在于曝光资源是否失衡。
