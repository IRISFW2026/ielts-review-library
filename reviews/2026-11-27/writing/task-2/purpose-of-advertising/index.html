1127-写作-大作文-The Purpose of Advertising 广告的目的

观点题 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

The only purpose of advertising is to encourage people to buy more. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

观点题观点题

01 审题判断

题型：观点题

核心立场：不同意“唯一目的”。促销是重要目的，但广告也传递产品信息、建立品牌认知、推广公共信息。

审题关键词：不同意“唯一目的”。促销是重要目的，但广告也传递产品信息、建立品牌认知、推广公共信息。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 促销：刺激需求、突出差异、促成购买，是商业广告的重要目标。
  • 信息：介绍新产品、价格、功能和服务，帮助消费者比较。
  • 公共价值：健康、安全、环保等公益宣传不以销售为直接目标。

03 考场作答指南

  • 抓住 only，必须回应绝对化论断。
  • 商业广告与公益广告分开举例，避免概念混淆。
  • 不要把“告知信息”写成与销售完全无关；说明它也可能帮助理性选择。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。
  • 每个主体段遵循“观点 → 解释 → 具体例子／结果”，避免只罗列观点。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 回应“唯一目的”这一绝对表述，表明不同意。

Body 1

→ 承认商业广告鼓励消费的作用，解释企业如何争取市场份额。

Body 2

→ 论证信息传播与公共宣传等其他目的，举例说明。

Conclusion

→ 总结销售是主要目标之一，但不是所有广告的唯一目的。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向；考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

Introduction

Advertising is commonly used by businesses to persuade consumers to purchase goods and services. However, I disagree that encouraging people to buy more is its only purpose, since advertising can also provide information and serve public interests.

Body 1

There is no doubt that increasing sales is a central objective of commercial advertising. Companies compete for customers, and advertisements allow them to present a product as more attractive than competing options. By highlighting convenience, quality or a special offer, businesses can stimulate consumer demand and encourage people to try products they might not otherwise consider. Advertising also helps new brands enter a market by raising awareness among potential buyers. In this sense, it is an important part of commercial competition and economic activity.

Body 2

Nevertheless, not all advertising is designed simply to increase consumption. One function is to provide information. For example, an advertisement for a new mobile phone may explain its battery life, price and technical features, enabling consumers to compare alternatives before making a decision. More importantly, public organisations use advertising to communicate messages that are not connected with selling products. Campaigns about road safety, vaccination or energy conservation aim to change behaviour and reduce social risks. Their success is measured by public understanding and safer choices rather than by sales figures.

Conclusion

In conclusion, encouraging purchases is a major purpose of commercial advertising, but it is not the only one. Advertising also informs consumers and helps governments and organisations communicate important public messages.
