1127-写作-大作文-Maths or Communication in Real Life 数学与沟通的现实价值

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 观点题 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Mathematical skills are more important in real life than communication skills. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

观点题

教育

审题判断

题目比较两种能力在现实生活中的重要性。不要把数学限定为高等数学，也不要把沟通缩窄为口才；应比较它们的适用范围和具体功能。

建议立场

部分不同意：数学能力在财务、技术和数据判断中不可替代，但沟通能力几乎贯穿所有职业与日常关系；两者的重要性取决于情境，不能简单排出绝对高低。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 数学：预算、贷款利息、测量、数据解读，帮助人们作出准确决定。
  • 沟通：表达需求、协商冲突、团队协作、建立信任，影响几乎所有职业。
  • 立场：基础数学是必要工具，但沟通能力的适用面更广；两者互补。

本题考场提醒

  • 用具体生活场景比较能力，而非抽象宣称谁更重要。
  • 主体段采用“能力—使用场景—实际结果”链条，避免只罗列职业。
  • 结论回到互补关系，不要突然改成两者完全同等。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

改写两种能力的比较，并表明不同意数学在所有现实情境中都更重要。

Body 1

承认数学的实际价值：个人财务、消费比较、数据判断；举生活化例子。

Body 2

论证沟通能力的广泛作用：工作协作、解决分歧、公共服务；指出其跨职业适用性。

Conclusion

总结数学提供准确计算，沟通推动合作，现实生活需要两者结合。

分段逻辑

先完成题目要求的两项核心任务，再用两个主体段分别展开不同论证方向；每段采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条，避免重复。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Although mathematics is essential in many practical situations, I disagree that it is more important than communication in every aspect of everyday life. The two skills serve different purposes, and communication is often needed across a wider range of situations.

Body 1

Mathematical ability enables people to handle routine decisions accurately. For example, individuals need to calculate household expenses, compare loan interest rates and understand discounts before making purchases. In many occupations, the ability to interpret numerical information is also important, particularly in fields such as engineering, finance and healthcare. Without basic numeracy, people may make costly mistakes or misunderstand information presented in figures. Therefore, mathematics is clearly a fundamental life skill.

Body 2

Nevertheless, communication is relevant to almost everyone, regardless of their profession. Employees must explain ideas, listen to colleagues and negotiate solutions when disagreements arise. Even people working in highly technical fields need to present their findings in a way that others can understand. Outside work, communication allows individuals to express their needs, maintain relationships and resolve misunderstandings. A person may calculate a budget correctly, for instance, but still struggle to manage shared household finances if they cannot discuss spending priorities with family members. This demonstrates that numerical ability alone is insufficient.

Conclusion

In conclusion, mathematics helps people make accurate calculations and informed decisions, whereas communication enables cooperation in both professional and personal life. I therefore believe that neither skill should be regarded as universally more important; both are essential and complementary.

本题复盘重点

本题的关键不是证明某一能力完全胜出，而是比较适用范围。数学段用财务与数据场景，沟通段用跨职业协作与人际关系，形成有层次的比较。
