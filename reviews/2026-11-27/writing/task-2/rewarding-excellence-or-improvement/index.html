1127-写作-大作文-Rewarding Excellence or Improvement 奖励优秀还是进步

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Some people think that only the best students should be rewarded. Others, however, think it is more important to reward students who show improvements. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

双边讨论

教育

审题判断

比较奖励结果与奖励进步两种标准。避免把“优秀学生”描写成不努力，也不要把“进步”理解为不需要达到基本标准。

建议立场

更支持奖励进步，同时保留对优秀成绩的认可；进步型奖励覆盖面更广，也能鼓励持续努力。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 奖励优秀：认可高水平表现，形成学术标杆，鼓励卓越。
  • 奖励进步：让起点不同的学生都看到努力有回报，增强动机。
  • 立场：学校可设置不同类别奖励，但进步奖励应更普遍，评价需看个人起点和持续努力。

本题考场提醒

  • 说明奖励标准分别激励什么行为：高成就 vs 持续努力。
  • 指出进步评价需考虑学生起点，避免简单比较分数涨幅。
  • 给出兼容方案：学术卓越奖与个人进步奖并存。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

说明两种奖励标准，并表明更重视进步型奖励。

Body 1

解释奖励优秀的公平性和示范作用。

Body 2

论证奖励进步的激励范围与持续性，并说明如何设定客观标准。

Conclusion

建议兼顾两类奖励，但将进步作为更广泛的激励方式。

分段逻辑

先完成题目要求的两项核心任务，再用两个主体段分别展开不同论证方向；每段采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条，避免重复。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Schools use rewards to encourage students, but there is debate over whether these should go only to the highest achievers or also to those who make progress. While excellent performance deserves recognition, I believe rewarding improvement is more beneficial for the student population as a whole.

Body 1

Giving awards to the best students has clear advantages. It recognises outstanding achievement and can set a high standard for others to follow. Students who consistently perform well may have invested considerable time and effort in their studies, and rewarding them signals that academic excellence is valued. Such recognition can also motivate ambitious learners to maintain their performance. In competitive subjects or examinations, clear achievement-based criteria may appear fair because students are judged against the same academic standards.

Body 2

Nevertheless, focusing only on top performers risks overlooking students who work hard but start from a lower level. A learner who improves from a weak result to an average one may have made substantial progress, even if they are still not among the highest-scoring students. Recognising this development can strengthen confidence and encourage further effort. It may be particularly important for students who have previously struggled or lacked educational support at home. To make such awards fair, schools should assess improvement in relation to individual starting points, consistency and the quality of work, rather than simply rewarding the largest numerical increase.

Conclusion

In conclusion, high achievers should be recognised for their results, but progress-based rewards are a more inclusive way to motivate students. Schools should offer both forms of recognition while placing greater emphasis on sustained improvement.

本题复盘重点

本题容易写成“努力比成绩重要”的口号。用起点不同解释为什么进步值得奖励，再补充评价标准，论证会更具体。
