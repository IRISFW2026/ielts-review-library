1127-写作-大作文-Watching Films and TV Alone 独自观看影视节目

两问作文 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

People these days prefer to watch films and TV programmes alone rather than with other people. Why is that? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

两问作文两问作文

01 审题判断

题型：两问作文

核心立场：原因主要是点播便利、观看习惯个性化和时间安排不同；总体中性偏负面，便利性积极，但若替代共同活动会削弱交流。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 原因：流媒体随时点播；个人口味不同；工作学习时间不一致；手机和平板便于个人观看。
  • 积极：自由选择内容和时间；适合不同兴趣与作息；避免迁就。
  • 消极：减少共同体验和讨论；家庭成员各自看屏幕，面对面交流减少。

03 考场作答指南

  • 第一问解释趋势成因，第二问单独评价影响，避免混写。
  • 把平台技术与生活节奏作为不同原因。
  • 评价时使用“取决于是否挤占共同活动”的条件判断。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 概括独自观看的趋势，并简要回应原因与总体评价。

Body 1

→ 解释点播平台、个性化推荐和时间安排差异如何推动独自观看。

Body 2

→ 先承认自主选择的便利，再论证共同观看减少带来的社交损失。

Conclusion

→ 总结其便利性，但强调不应完全取代共享娱乐。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向，考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

完整全文

Introduction

More people now watch films and television programmes on their own. This is largely due to flexible streaming services and increasingly individual lifestyles. Although this habit offers convenience, I believe it can have negative social effects if it replaces shared activities.

Body 1

A major reason is the growth of on-demand platforms. Viewers no longer need to follow a fixed television schedule or agree on one programme with other family members. They can pause, replay or select content at any time, which makes individual viewing attractive. Personal preferences also differ: one person may enjoy documentaries while another prefers drama or sport. In addition, work and study schedules are often irregular, so friends and relatives may not have free time at the same moment. Watching alone is therefore a practical way to enjoy entertainment without arranging a group activity.

Body 2

This trend has some advantages. Individuals can choose content that matches their interests and relax at a time that suits them. It may also reduce disagreements about what to watch. However, films and television can provide shared experiences that encourage conversation and strengthen relationships. Family members who always watch separate screens may lose opportunities to discuss stories, laugh together or spend time with one another. This is particularly concerning when individual viewing becomes the default form of leisure rather than an occasional choice. People can preserve the benefits of personal choice by setting aside some time for shared programmes or other social activities.

Conclusion

In conclusion, flexible technology and different schedules explain why people increasingly watch media alone. While this offers freedom and convenience, it may weaken everyday interaction if it entirely replaces shared entertainment.
