1127-写作-大作文-Coaching or Natural Talent in Sport 教练与运动天赋

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

In order to be successful in sport, some people think it is essential to have a good coach. Others say that athletes’ natural ability is more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

双边讨论

健康与生活

审题判断

讨论成功来自教练还是天赋。需要区分先天潜能与后天表现，并说明两者如何相互作用。

建议立场

天赋提供起点和潜力，但高水平表现更依赖长期训练与专业指导；教练帮助运动员把潜力转化为稳定成绩。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 天赋：身体条件、反应速度、协调性和耐力可能使运动员更容易达到高水平。
  • 教练：制定训练计划、纠正动作、控制负荷、提供战术和心理指导，减少受伤风险。
  • 立场：天赋有帮助，但训练与教练决定潜力能否持续发展。

本题考场提醒

  • 不要把天赋等同于成功，强调它只是起点。
  • 教练段写具体作用：技术反馈、训练周期、战术、伤病预防。
  • 观点要比较“潜能”和“长期表现”的关系。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

引出天赋与教练的争议，并表明教练和训练更能决定长期成功。

Body 1

解释自然能力如何提供竞争优势和学习基础。

Body 2

说明教练如何系统发展能力、改进技术并管理风险。

Conclusion

总结天赋提供潜力，专业指导使潜力转化为成绩。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Some people believe that sporting success depends mainly on having a good coach, while others regard natural ability as more important. Although talent can provide an initial advantage, I believe professional coaching and consistent training play the greater role in achieving lasting success.

Body 1

Natural ability can certainly make a difference in sport. Athletes may be born with physical advantages such as speed, strength, coordination or endurance. These characteristics can make it easier for them to learn particular movements and compete at a high level. A naturally fast runner, for instance, may have greater potential in sprint events than someone who lacks similar physical attributes. Talent can also help athletes respond quickly to training and perform well under pressure. It is therefore reasonable to argue that natural ability provides an important foundation.

Body 2

However, talent alone rarely produces consistent results. Athletes need structured training programmes that develop strength, technique and tactical understanding over time. A good coach can identify weaknesses, correct inefficient movements and adjust training intensity to prevent injury. Coaches also provide feedback and psychological support, helping athletes remain focused after setbacks. Even a gifted player may fail to reach their full potential if they train incorrectly or lack discipline. By contrast, an athlete with average natural ability may achieve a high standard through careful preparation and sustained effort. This is why coaching is particularly important as competition becomes more demanding.

Conclusion

In conclusion, natural talent offers a valuable starting point, but it does not guarantee success. I believe good coaching and disciplined training are more important because they turn potential into reliable performance.

本题复盘重点

核心区分是潜能与成绩。天赋段承认先天优势，教练段解释如何把优势转化为技术、战术和稳定发挥。
