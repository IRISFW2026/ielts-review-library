1127-写作-大作文-Who Should Fund Public Art? 公共艺术应由谁资助

双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

Some people think that public art should be funded by governments, while others believe that it should be funded by other sources. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

双边讨论双边讨论

01 审题判断

题型：双边讨论

核心立场：政府应承担基础性资助，确保公共可及性和地区公平；私人赞助、基金会和社区筹款可补充资金，但不应决定全部艺术方向。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 政府资助：保障免费或低价接触艺术；支持偏远地区和商业回报低的项目；保护文化遗产。
  • 其他来源：减轻财政负担；引入专业资源和创新；企业赞助可能带来品牌影响或偏好干预。
  • 立场：公共财政提供基本保障，多元资金补充；资助规则公开透明。

03 考场作答指南

  • 解释公共艺术为何具有公共价值：社区共享、教育、文化身份。
  • 不要把私人资金全部写成坏处，承认资金与专业资源优势。
  • 提出政府兜底与私人补充的分工，并强调透明规则。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 引出资助来源争议，并提出政府基础资助、多元资金补充的立场。

Body 1

→ 解释政府资助如何保障公平、公共可及性和长期文化项目。

Body 2

→ 讨论私人赞助的灵活性与商业干预风险，提出多元资助和透明治理。

Conclusion

→ 总结政府应兜底，其他来源可补充。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向，考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

完整全文

Introduction

There is debate over whether public art should be financed by governments or by private organisations and individuals. I believe governments should provide the basic level of funding because public art has cultural and educational value, while other sources can offer useful additional support.

Body 1

Government funding can ensure that art is available to people regardless of their income or where they live. Public sculptures, community murals and exhibitions in civic spaces can be enjoyed without expensive admission fees. Governments can also support projects that have limited commercial appeal but are important to local identity or cultural heritage. For example, an artwork in a small town may attract few tourists, yet it can still strengthen residents' sense of belonging. Public funding is therefore important for maintaining access and ensuring that cultural opportunities are not concentrated only in wealthy areas.

Body 2

On the other hand, relying entirely on government budgets may restrict the number of projects that can be supported, especially when public finances are under pressure. Businesses, charities and private donors can provide additional money, professional expertise and opportunities for collaboration. However, commercial sponsors may expect their brand to receive attention or may prefer projects that attract large audiences. This could limit artistic independence. A balanced system would allow private contributions while requiring transparent agreements and public oversight. In this way, sponsors can support projects without controlling their content or excluding less profitable forms of art.

Conclusion

In conclusion, private and charitable funding can broaden the resources available for public art, but governments should remain responsible for ensuring fair access and protecting cultural value. A combination of public funding and transparent private support is the most suitable approach.
