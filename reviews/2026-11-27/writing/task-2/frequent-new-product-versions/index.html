1127-写作-大作文-Frequent New Product Versions 产品频繁推出新版本

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 正负面发展 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Nowadays companies make many new versions of products. Do you think it is a positive or negative phenomenon?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

正负面发展

工作与商业

审题判断

题目讨论频繁推出新版本这一现象。应比较创新、选择和产品改进，与资源浪费、消费压力和电子垃圾等后果。

建议立场

总体利大于弊，但前提是更新带来实质改进，而不是制造人为淘汰和不必要消费。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 积极：技术迭代改善安全、性能和能源效率；消费者有更多选择；企业竞争推动创新。
  • 消极：小幅升级诱导频繁更换；维修和兼容性问题；增加资源消耗与电子垃圾。
  • 立场：实质创新有价值，需通过耐用设计、维修权和透明升级信息减少负面影响。

本题考场提醒

  • 区分“推出新版本”与“消费者必须更换”，避免把二者混为一谈。
  • 积极段写具体改进（性能、安全、能耗），不要只说科技进步。
  • 消极段形成“诱导更换—旧品废弃—资源与环境成本”因果链。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

说明频繁更新的趋势，并表明总体积极但需避免人为淘汰。

Body 1

解释真正创新如何改善产品性能、安全和选择。

Body 2

讨论过度更新带来的消费与环境成本，并提出责任措施。

Conclusion

重申创新本身积极，问题在于缺乏实质价值的频繁更换。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

04 高分表达 · 翻卡复习

Phrase card

熟练度

本题表达清单

1

bring meaningful improvements

带来实质性改进

A new version should bring meaningful improvements rather than cosmetic changes.

2

extend the product life cycle

延长产品生命周期

Repair services can extend the product life cycle.

3

encourage unnecessary replacement

促使不必要的更换

Frequent minor upgrades may encourage unnecessary replacement.

4

generate electronic waste

产生电子垃圾

Discarded devices generate electronic waste that is difficult to process.

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Companies increasingly release updated versions of products, from mobile phones to household appliances. I believe this is generally a positive trend because competition can improve quality and choice, although frequent upgrades become harmful when they encourage unnecessary replacement.

Body 1

Regular product development can bring practical benefits to consumers. New versions may offer better safety features, longer battery life or more efficient use of energy. These improvements can make products more reliable and reduce running costs. Competition between manufacturers also gives consumers a wider range of options, allowing them to choose goods that suit their needs and budgets. In addition, the possibility of launching improved products encourages companies to invest in research and respond to customer feedback. When innovation solves genuine problems, frequent updates can raise living standards.

Body 2

However, some companies release new versions with only minor changes and use advertising to persuade customers that older models are no longer desirable. This can create unnecessary spending, particularly when devices remain fully functional. Replacing products too often also increases demand for raw materials and generates electronic waste, which may contain substances that require careful disposal. These disadvantages can be reduced if manufacturers provide longer software support, make spare parts available and design products that can be repaired. Consumers should also compare the actual improvements before buying a new model.

Conclusion

Overall, frequent product updates are beneficial when they deliver meaningful improvements. The negative effects arise mainly from upgrades that encourage wasteful replacement, so innovation should be accompanied by durable design and responsible consumption.

本题复盘重点

不要把频繁更新直接等同于频繁购买。将企业推出版本和消费者更换产品分开讨论，论证更准确。
