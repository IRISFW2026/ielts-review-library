1127-写作-大作文-Is Money the Best Workplace Motivator? 金钱是最大的工作动力吗

双边讨论 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

Many factors motivate people in the workplace. Money is the most important factor. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

双边讨论双边讨论

01 审题判断

题型：双边讨论

核心立场：金钱是基础且重要的动力，尤其关系到生活保障；但长期工作投入还取决于认可、发展、 autonomy 和良好环境。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 支持金钱：满足生活成本、反映劳动价值、吸引人才并减少经济压力。
  • 反对金钱唯一/最重要：尊重、晋升、技能成长、工作自主权影响长期满意度。
  • 立场：薪酬是底线和重要因素，但公平薪酬之后，非金钱因素决定持续动力。

03 考场作答指南

  • 区分短期激励与长期投入，避免简单列举因素。
  • 解释薪酬的基础作用：生活成本与公平回报。
  • 论证非金钱因素如何提高归属感、成长和主动性。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 指出薪酬的重要性，同时表明它并非对所有员工都最重要。

Body 1

→ 解释收入如何提供生活保障并形成直接激励。

Body 2

→ 讨论认可、成长与自主性如何形成长期投入；明确自己的排序。

Conclusion

→ 总结公平薪酬是基础，持续动力来自多种因素。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向，考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

Introduction

People are motivated at work by a range of factors, including salary, recognition and opportunities for development. Although I consider money essential, I do not believe it is always the most important factor, particularly once employees receive fair pay.

Body 1

A good salary is a powerful motivator because it provides financial security. Employees need sufficient income to meet housing, food and family expenses, and a higher salary can reward responsibility or specialised skills. Pay also influences whether people apply for a job or remain with an employer. If workers believe that their effort is not fairly compensated, they may become dissatisfied and look for better opportunities elsewhere. For this reason, employers cannot expect staff to remain motivated if wages are inadequate or clearly below the market level.

Body 2

However, money alone may not create lasting commitment. Many employees also want to feel valued, develop their abilities and have some control over how they work. For example, a skilled employee may prefer a role that offers training and a realistic promotion path to a slightly better-paid position with no opportunity to progress. Respectful management and a supportive working environment can also reduce stress and encourage people to contribute ideas. In my view, once a fair level of compensation has been established, these non-financial factors often determine whether employees remain engaged over time.

Conclusion

In conclusion, money is fundamental because it provides security and recognises employees' work, but it is not the only source of motivation. Fair pay combined with professional development, recognition and good management is more effective than salary alone.
