1127-写作-大作文-Access to Communication Technology 通信技术的普及与数字鸿沟

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 观点题 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Some people benefit from modern communication technology, but some people still do not have access to it. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

观点题

科技与媒体

审题判断

题干给出一个现象并要求表态。重点是确认现代通信技术确实让部分人受益，而另一部分人被排除；解释差距形成机制和影响。

建议立场

同意这一判断：技术收益并未均匀分配，网络覆盖、设备成本、数字技能和残障可及性都会造成差距。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 受益者：远程学习、医疗咨询、求职、商业联系和紧急信息获取更方便。
  • 无法接入者：偏远地区网络不足、设备和流量昂贵、数字技能不足、老年人或残障人士面临使用障碍。
  • 结果：获取教育、就业和公共服务的机会不平等。

本题考场提醒

  • 不要把“没有设备”和“有设备但不会使用”混为一谈。
  • 主体段要从接入差异推到机会差异，而非只说不方便。
  • 可用公共网络、可负担设备和数字培训回应不同障碍。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

同意数字收益分配不均，并概括基础设施与能力两类原因。

Body 1

说明接入者如何通过通信技术获得学习、工作和服务机会。

Body 2

解释未接入者的障碍及其社会后果，提出普惠接入方向。

Conclusion

重申技术进步必须与可及性政策同步。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Modern communication technology has transformed the way people study, work and maintain relationships. However, I agree that its benefits are not shared equally, because access depends on infrastructure, affordability and people's ability to use digital services.

Body 1

For those who have reliable internet connections and suitable devices, communication technology creates considerable opportunities. Students can attend online courses, access digital libraries and communicate with teachers without travelling long distances. Workers can apply for jobs, collaborate remotely and maintain contact with colleagues in other locations. Small businesses can also reach customers beyond their local area through online platforms. In addition, digital communication makes it easier to obtain health information and receive urgent updates. These advantages can save time and make important services more accessible.

Body 2

Nevertheless, people in rural or low-income communities may lack stable internet access or be unable to afford computers and data plans. Even where a connection is available, some older people and those with limited digital literacy may find online systems difficult to use. As more public services move online, these groups risk being excluded from education, employment opportunities and essential information. Governments and service providers should therefore invest in reliable rural networks, offer affordable devices and provide practical digital training. Important services should also retain non-digital alternatives for people who cannot use online systems.

Conclusion

In conclusion, communication technology offers major benefits, but these are unevenly distributed. Bridging the digital divide requires both physical access and the skills needed to use technology effectively. Without such measures, technological progress may deepen existing inequalities instead of reducing them.

本题复盘重点

本题的重点是“不平等地受益”。把基础设施、经济能力、数字技能分成不同障碍，再说明它们如何影响机会。
