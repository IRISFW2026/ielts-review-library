1127-写作-大作文-Raising the Legal Driving Age 提高法定驾驶年龄

IELTS Writing Task 2 · 观点题 · 目标 Band 7–7.5

01 原题与审题

Original question · 原题

Some people say that the best way to improve road safety is to increase the minimum legal age for driving cars or riding motor bikes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

建议用时：40 分钟 · 不少于 250 词

观点题

交通与旅游

审题判断

关键词是 the best way。需要评价提高年龄是否优于其他措施，而不只是说明它有没有一点作用。

建议立场

不同意这是最佳办法。提高年龄可能降低部分年轻新手风险，但驾驶经验、培训、执法和道路设计更直接，也更全面。

02 解题思路与立意

核心论证方向

  • 可能有效：部分年轻驾驶者风险判断和冲动控制尚在发展；减少最年轻群体上路。
  • 局限：年龄不能代表个人能力；年龄更大也可能缺乏经验；延后驾驶影响偏远地区出行。
  • 更有效组合：分阶段驾照、强制训练、严格酒驾与超速执法、道路安全设计。

本题考场提醒

  • 回应 best，而不是只回答“提高年龄好不好”。
  • 年龄与驾驶能力不是一一对应，强调经验和行为因素。
  • 给出具体替代措施，并解释它们如何减少事故风险。

03 四段式写作规划

Introduction

承认提高年龄可能有局部作用，但否定其为最佳单一措施。

Body 1

解释年轻驾驶者风险及提高年龄可能带来的有限收益。

Body 2

指出年龄标准的局限，比较培训、分级驾照和执法等更直接措施。

Conclusion

总结综合安全政策比单纯提高年龄更有效。

分段逻辑

每个主体段集中解决一个论证方向，采用“观点—解释—例子/结果”的链条；避免在两个主体段重复同一理由。

05 范文与复盘

Band 7–7.5 model essay

以下为根据原题独立组织的示范范文，不是原文档中提供的考生作文。

Introduction

Raising the minimum legal age for driving is sometimes suggested as a way to make roads safer. Although this may reduce risks among the youngest drivers, I disagree that it is the best solution because driving behaviour, training and law enforcement are more direct factors.

Body 1

There are reasons why a higher minimum age might improve safety to some extent. Younger drivers often have limited experience in recognising hazards and may be more likely to take risks, particularly when travelling with friends. Delaying access to a full licence could reduce the number of inexperienced drivers on the road and give young people more time to develop judgement. This may be useful in countries where road accidents involving novice drivers are common.

Body 2

However, age alone is a poor measure of driving competence. Some young people are cautious and well trained, while older beginners may also lack practical experience. Raising the age would therefore delay driving for everyone in a particular group without ensuring that those who eventually receive licences are safe drivers. More effective policies would include a graduated licensing system, compulsory supervised practice and regular assessments of hazard perception. Governments should also enforce speed limits, seat-belt rules and drink-driving laws consistently. Safer road design, such as clearer crossings and protected lanes for motorcyclists, can reduce the consequences of mistakes made by all road users.

Conclusion

In conclusion, increasing the driving age may offer limited benefits, but it cannot address the full range of causes of road accidents. Better training, graduated licensing, law enforcement and safer infrastructure are more effective ways to improve road safety.

本题复盘重点

用“best way”作为判断标准：提高年龄只是筛掉一部分年轻驾驶者，无法保证获证者安全。与更直接的培训、执法和道路设计比较。
