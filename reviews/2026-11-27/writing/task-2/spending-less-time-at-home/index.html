1127-写作-大作文-Spending Less Time at Home 待在家的时间越来越少

两问作文 · 目标 Band 7–7.5 · 40 分钟

考察要点 · 原题与考场作答指南

Original question · 原题

Nowadays, people spend less and less time at home. What are the reasons for this? What are the effects of this on individuals and society?

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.

两问作文两问作文

01 审题判断

题型：两问作文

核心立场：原因包括工作与通勤、外出消费和休闲选择增多；影响有积极的社交与体验，也有家庭互动减少和社区关系弱化。

02 解题思路与立意

  • 原因：工作时间长、通勤；商场健身房餐饮等外部活动丰富；线上娱乐和社交也可在外部空间进行。
  • 个人影响：拓展社交、锻炼和体验；但休息减少、家庭照护和亲子交流受影响。
  • 社会影响：公共空间和服务业活跃；但家庭与邻里联系减弱，照护压力可能转移。

03 考场作答指南

  • 原因与影响分开写，不要在第一主体段提前展开后果。
  • 影响题要明确写出 individuals 和 society 两个层面。
  • 避免把所有外出都写成坏事，评价要体现不同活动的不同结果。
  • 先用约 5 分钟完成审题与提纲，再按四段结构展开论证。

04 四段式写作规划 · 写提纲

Introduction

→ 改写在家时间减少的现象，并提示原因与多层面影响。

Body 1

→ 解释工作节奏和外部休闲选择如何减少居家时间。

Body 2

→ 分析个人层面的社交机会与家庭时间损失，再扩展到社区和公共生活。

Conclusion

→ 总结多重原因及利弊并存的影响。

每段只保留核心论点和论证方向，考场上据此扩展成完整段落。

完整全文

Introduction

People in many societies now spend less of their free time at home than in the past. This is mainly caused by changing work patterns and the growth of outside leisure activities, and it can bring both opportunities and social costs.

Body 1

One important reason is that modern working life often requires people to spend long hours away from home. Some employees work overtime, while others travel considerable distances between their homes and workplaces. As a result, a large part of the day is spent outside before leisure time even begins. Another reason is that cities provide a wider range of activities, including restaurants, gyms, shopping centres and entertainment venues. People may prefer to meet friends, exercise or study in these places rather than remain at home. Higher incomes and more flexible consumer services have made such choices increasingly accessible.

Body 2

For individuals, spending time outside can be beneficial because it creates opportunities to socialise, exercise and experience new activities. However, it may also reduce the time available for rest and family responsibilities. Parents who return home late, for example, may have fewer chances to talk with their children or share meals. At a social level, busy public spaces and leisure businesses can support local economies and create jobs. On the other hand, if people rarely interact with family members or neighbours, informal support networks may become weaker. This can make it harder for communities to care for older residents or families in difficulty.

Conclusion

In conclusion, longer working hours and greater choice of outside activities explain why people spend less time at home. The trend can enrich social and leisure life, but a reasonable balance is needed to protect family relationships and community connections.
