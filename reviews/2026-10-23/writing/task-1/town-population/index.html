柱状图

考察要点

Overview · 写期末净增长和年龄组更替

总人口在 1991 年后回落，2011 年仍高于 1951 年。最大年龄组从 40–64 岁变为 65 岁及以上。

The population grew overall, while those aged 65 and over became the largest age group.

Details 1 · 写峰值和净变化

10 万 → 15 万 → 20 万 → 约 17.5 万。峰值是起点两倍，期末仍高出起点约 75%。

The population doubled to 200,000 before falling to about 175,000.

Details 2 · 按趋势组合年龄组

40–64 岁约 45%→21%，65+ 约 10%→25%；两组反向且排名互换。再简写两组年轻人口上升和 30–39 岁回落。

The share of 40–64-year-olds fell markedly, whereas the proportion aged 65 and over more than doubled.

1

先翻倍后回落，仍高于起点

It therefore doubled over forty years before falling to about 175,000 in 2011, leaving the final figure 75% higher than its initial level in 1951.

因此，它（该镇总人口）在四十年间翻了一倍，随后在2011年降至约175,000，使最终人口数比1951年的初始水平高出75%。

句式骨架
[数值] doubled over [时间] before falling to [终值], leaving it [差值] higher than its initial level.

Sales doubled over five years before falling to 150,000 units, leaving them 50% higher than their initial level of 100,000.

销量在五年内翻倍，随后回落至 15 万件，仍比起点 10 万件高 50%。

Visitor numbers doubled over a decade before falling to 18,000, leaving them 80% higher than their initial level of 10,000.

游客数量在十年内翻倍，随后回落至 1.8 万，仍比最初的 1 万高 80%。

Output doubled over twenty years before falling to 30,000 tonnes, leaving it 50% higher than its initial level of 20,000 tonnes.

产量在二十年内翻倍，随后降到 3 万吨，仍比最初的 2 万吨高 50%。

2

一组明显下降，另一组逆势上升

The share of residents aged 40–64 fell from about 45% to 21%, whereas the proportion aged 65 and over rose from 10% to 25%.

40–64岁居民的占比从约45%降至21%，而65岁及以上人群的占比从10%升至25%。

句式骨架
The share of [A] fell from [X]% to [Y]%, whereas the proportion of [B] rose from [M]% to [N]%.

The share of journeys made by car fell from 45% to 30%, whereas the proportion made by bus rose from 20% to 32%.

私家车出行占比从 45% 降至 30%，公交则从 20% 升至 32%。

The share of electricity generated from coal fell from 50% to 32%, whereas the proportion generated from wind rose from 8% to 25%.

煤电份额从 50% 降至 32%，风电份额则从 8% 升至 25%。

The share of visitors under 25 fell from 36% to 25%, whereas the proportion aged 65 and over rose from 12% to 22%.

25 岁以下游客的占比从 36% 降至 25%，65 岁及以上的占比则从 12% 升至 22%。
